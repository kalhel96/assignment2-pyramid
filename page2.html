Frequently Asked Questions

People have been fascinated by the pyramids for thousands of years. Here are answers to a few of the most common questions.

Despite many myths, historians agree the pyramids were built by skilled paid workers, not slaves, who lived in nearby villages.

Quick Facts

  • The Great Pyramid used about 2.3 million stone blocks.
  • It took an estimated 20 years to build.
  • The pyramids are aligned almost perfectly with the compass points.
