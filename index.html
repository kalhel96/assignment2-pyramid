What Are the Pyramids?

The Egyptian pyramids are massive stone structures built as tombs for pharaohs and their queens. Most were constructed during the Old and Middle Kingdom periods, over 4,000 years ago.

The largest and most famous is the Great Pyramid of Giza, built for the pharaoh Khufu. It was the tallest human-made structure in the world for nearly 4,000 years.

Most Famous Pyramids

  • Great Pyramid of Giza (Khufu)
  • Pyramid of Khafre
  • Pyramid of Menkaure

Steps to Build a Pyramid

  1. Choose and level the ground
  2. Quarry and cut the giant stone blocks
  3. Drag and ramp the blocks into place
  4. Add the smooth outer casing stones
